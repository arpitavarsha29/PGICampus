# Prasad Group of Institutions (Jaunpur, UP) — College Photographs Folder

Place your authentic photographs of the Prasad Group of Institutions campus in this directory.

### Supported Image Filenames:
1. **`pgi_main_campus_building.jpg`** (Primary background)
   - Authentic photograph of the main administrative entrance & campus facade of PGI, Jaunpur.
   - Recommended resolution: 1920x1080 or higher (16:9 aspect ratio).

2. **`pgi_academic_block.jpg`**
   - Authentic photograph of the PIT Engineering & Technology academic building and lecture halls.

3. **`pgi_library_hall.jpg`**
   - Central library, reading halls, and study areas.

4. **`pgi_laboratories.jpg`**
   - Advanced computer science labs, mechanical / electrical workshops, and pharmacy laboratories.

5. **`pgi_green_campus.jpg`**
   - Campus lawns, sports grounds, and green courtyard areas.

### How it works:
- The website displays the photograph as a responsive full-screen background with `background-size: cover` and `background-position: center`.
- A 70% white overlay is applied via a separate background layer, rendering the college photograph at approximately 30% visibility for readability of all dashboard cards, text, and tables.
- You can switch between active campus views using the "Campus View" selector in the top bar / footer.
